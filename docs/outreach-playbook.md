# Outreach Playbook

## Purpose

Use this playbook to keep host candidate outreach small, respectful, and auditable. The cockpit can generate drafts, but a person must approve every first-contact wave.

## Message Template Rules

- Use category-specific templates when available.
- Use the default template only when the category has no tailored version.
- Keep first contact short.
- State why the candidate is relevant.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, pressure, or fake familiarity.
- Include a clear opt-out sentence.
- Do not mention compensation unless the campaign owner has approved the terms.

## Template Review Levels

- `brand`: tone, positioning, and beGlib fit.
- `privacy`: source disclosure, opt-out language, and data minimization.
- `commercial`: compensation, revenue share, paid promotion, or sponsorship terms.
- `sensitive-category`: religion, politics, health, vulnerable communities, or other high-care contexts.
- `legal`: rights, contracts, privacy requests, complaints, or jurisdiction-specific issues.

## First Pilot Wave

Recommended starting wave:

- 10 to 25 candidates
- One or two categories only
- Public professional or representative contact routes only
- Manual approval for every message
- Manual review for every reply
- No automated follow-up until opt-out handling is verified

Suggested first categories:

- AI and education researchers
- Science communicators
- Developer educators
- Long-form interview podcasters

## Pre-Send Checklist

- Candidate has a public professional or representative contact route.
- Source URL is stored.
- Verification date is current.
- Compliance assessment is `Ready` or manually approved after review.
- Message includes sender identity and opt-out language.
- Suppression list has been checked.
- No real lead data is committed to GitHub.

## Stop Conditions

Pause a candidate or campaign when:

- A person opts out.
- A person asks how their data was obtained.
- A person asks for deletion.
- A complaint arrives.
- A message involves legal, rights, privacy, compensation, religious, political, or health-sensitive context.
- Deliverability signals degrade.
